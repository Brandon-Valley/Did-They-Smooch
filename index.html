Rules & credits

A smooch is a performed lip-to-lip kiss in a released movie or show. Cheek kisses and private relationships don't count. NO means no documented example found; it is not proof of a negative. The movie-specific modes ask only about the named production or the listed examples.

Correct rounds earn 100 points plus up to 100 streak points. Tap one of the two portraits to answer Matchmaker. There is no second confirmation step. Once a question is displayed, its featured pairing is marked seen in both round types. Distractors are not counted as played questions.
